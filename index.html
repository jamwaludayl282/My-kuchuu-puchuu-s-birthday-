A LITTLE SOMETHING FOR YOU

You already know our story...

But maybe you've never seen it the way I felt it.

From 2018 to you...

2018

BEFORE YOU KNEW ME

The First Time
I Saw You

Garima, I don't know if you remember 2018 the way I do.

We were in our first year of college. I knew you existed. I knew your name was Garima, and I knew that we were in the same year.

And honestly... that's about it.

You probably didn't even know who I was.

There was no conversation, no friendship, no story between us.

Just a girl I had noticed somewhere in college.

And a boy who had absolutely no idea that this girl would eventually become such an important part of his life.

2020

THE CRUSH BEGINS

When You Became
My Crush

Somewhere around 2020, I found my way to your social media.

I followed you.

And slowly, I started watching your posts, your reels, your little updates...

Mostly from a distance.

At first, I genuinely thought it was just an attraction.

You were pretty, I liked you, and that was probably all it was.

Or at least, that's what I told myself.

But as time passed, I started noticing something.

The way I felt about you didn't feel the same as the way I felt about anyone else.

I didn't really understand it.

I just knew that somehow, you were different.

Instagram Era

I started commenting on your posts and reels.

Not expecting anything... 😂

I'd hide my actual feelings inside cute, funny and slightly romantic comments.

Not because I expected anything.

Honestly, I didn't even expect you to reply.

I was just happy that somehow I could put a tiny piece of what I felt into words.

And then...

You started replying.

😭❤️

And thank God...

You didn't find me creepy. 😂

2023

Then came 2023.

And among them was...

Sakshi & Shaili ❤️

Just slowly...

2024

THE FIRST CONFESSION

The First Time
I Actually Said It

Then came Snapchat.

We started talking.

And after around two months, I finally did something that the 2018 version of me would probably never have believed possible.

I finally...

told you how I felt.

And...

It didn't go the way I had hoped.

At the time, I genuinely thought I had been rejected.

And yeah...

It hurt. A lot.

But strangely, there was also a little happiness somewhere inside me.

Because no matter what your answer was...

I had finally told you.

For someone as introverted as me, and especially for someone who would get nervous just being around you, that was a huge thing.

I had confronted my biggest fear.

I had finally told the girl I had liked for years how I felt.

So after that, I decided to back off a little.

I didn't want you to think I was being creepy by continuing to talk to you after what I thought was a rejection.

I thought maybe this was where my little story ended.

But apparently...

It wasn't.

Maybe I shouldn't give up just because I was scared.

SOMETHING FROM MY HEART

A Letter For You

My Love,

If someone had told the 2018 version of me that one day I would be sitting here, writing a birthday letter for you as my girlfriend, I probably wouldn't have believed them.

Back then, you were just the girl I knew existed. The girl whose name I knew. The girl who was in the same year as me.

You probably didn't even know who I was. And somehow, without either of us knowing it, you were already becoming a small part of my story.

Then came 2020.

You became the girl whose pictures I would quietly look at, whose posts I would wait for, whose reels somehow always caught my attention.

At first I thought it was just attraction. I thought maybe it was just a crush that would eventually fade.

But it didn't.

The more I knew about you, the more I realised that there was something about you that I couldn't explain. Something that made you different from everyone else.

And the funny thing is... even after all these years, I still can't completely explain it.

I just know that when it comes to you, my heart has always felt something that it never felt for anyone else.

I used to hide my feelings inside silly comments, little jokes and random compliments because I was too nervous to actually tell you what was going on in my head.

I never expected those comments to become conversations. I never expected you to reply. And I definitely never imagined that one day I would actually get to call you mine.

There were moments when I genuinely thought that maybe this would never happen.

There was a time when I thought I had been rejected. A time when I decided to step back because I didn't want to make you uncomfortable.

But somehow, life gave us another chance.

And I'm so grateful that it did.

Because if I had given up completely that day, I don't know where we would be today.

I don't know if you realise how much your presence has changed my life.

You didn't just become my girlfriend.

You became someone whose presence makes ordinary days feel special.

Someone whose messages can change my entire mood.

Someone whose smile can make me forget whatever was bothering me.

Someone I want to tell everything to.

Someone whose happiness genuinely matters to me.

And someone I feel incredibly lucky to have in my life.

I am grateful for every little thing about you.

I'm grateful for the way you talk to me. I'm grateful for the way you make me comfortable. I'm grateful for the way you calm me down when I'm nervous. I'm grateful for all the little conversations, all the jokes, all the memories and all the moments that might look small to someone else but mean so much to me.

I'm grateful that you gave me a chance to know you beyond the girl I used to admire from a distance.

I'm grateful that I got to discover the person behind the girl I had a crush on.

And somehow... I ended up loving that person even more.

You have become such a beautiful part of my life that sometimes I genuinely wonder how I ever imagined my life without you in it.

There are so many things I love about you. Some of them I have probably told you, and some of them I probably never will be able to explain properly because words just don't feel enough.

I love your smile. I love the way you talk. I love your little expressions. I love the way you make me feel safe enough to be myself around you.

I love the little things you probably don't even notice about yourself.

And more than anything... I love the person I get to be when I'm with you.

You make me want to become a better version of myself. Not because you ever asked me to, but because when someone means this much to you, you naturally want to become someone worthy of them.

I don't want to love you only on the easy days.

I want to be there for your difficult days too. I want to celebrate your happiness, listen to your worries, support your dreams, make you laugh when you don't feel like smiling, and simply be there whenever you need someone.

I want to know every version of you. The happy you. The tired you. The excited you. The annoyed you. The emotional you. The completely random you.

I want to keep discovering you, even after years have passed.

Because loving you doesn't feel like something I want to finish.

It feels like something I want to keep choosing. Again and again. Every day.

And there's something else that still makes me laugh.

You were my crush in 2020.

You became the girl I fell in love with.

You became my girlfriend.

And somehow...

I still look at you like you're my crush. ❤️

Maybe that's never going to change.

Maybe I'll still get nervous around you sometimes. Maybe my heart will still beat a little faster when I see you unexpectedly.

And honestly... I hope that never changes.

Because I never want to stop feeling lucky that the girl I once thought I could never even talk to is now the girl whose hand I get to hold.

Garima, I don't know what the future looks like. I don't know what life will bring us, what challenges we'll face, or how much we'll both change over the years.

But I know one thing.

Whatever life looks like, I want you to know how deeply grateful I am that our paths crossed.

Out of all the people I could have met, somehow I met you.

Out of all the stories my life could have had, somehow I got ours.

And out of all the people I could have fallen for...

Somehow, it was you.

And if I had to choose again, knowing everything I know now, knowing all the nervous moments, the misunderstandings, the waiting, the fear, the happiness, the tears, the proposal, the hugs, and every little moment in between...

I would still choose you. ❤️

Without hesitation.

Because you are not just a chapter in my life.

You are one of the most beautiful parts of it.

And I hope you always know just how loved you are.

I hope you know that somewhere in this world, there is a boy who once admired you from a distance, who was too nervous to even look at you properly, who thought he would probably never get a chance with you...

And that boy is now incredibly grateful that he gets to love you.

Thank you for coming into my life.

Thank you for staying.

Thank you for understanding me.

Thank you for being you.

And thank you for giving me a love I once only imagined from a distance.

I love you more than I probably know how to put into words.

And no matter how many times I say it, I don't think I'll ever get tired of saying it.

I love you, Garima. ❤️

Happy Birthday, my love.

Here's to you. To us. To everything we've already lived. And to everything that's still waiting for us.

You were my favourite crush.

You are my love.

And somehow...

You'll always be my favourite girl. ❤️
